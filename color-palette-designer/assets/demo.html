Why OKLCH: HSL lightness lies

    
Same hue sweep, six hues. Row 1: hsl(h 80% 50%). Row 2: oklch(0.65 0.14 h). In grayscale the HSL row shows wildly different lightness (yellow bright, blue dark); the OKLCH row stays even, which is why contrast can be solved by moving L alone.
